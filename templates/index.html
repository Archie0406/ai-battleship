THE AI BEHIND THE BATTLE

Eight reasoning techniques work together to decide, hunt, and adapt in real time.

▦

CSP

Models fleet placement as a constraint satisfaction problem with no overlaps or contradictions.

⌥

Backtracking

Searches placement possibilities and retreats the instant a choice breaks a constraint.

▤

BFS

Explores the grid level by level to guarantee the shortest path to a target.

▥

DFS

Dives deep along one line of reasoning before backing out to try another.

✦

A*

Combines cost and heuristics to plan the most efficient route to a likely hit.

◎

Probability

Scores every unfired cell by how many consistent ship placements still cover it.

♟

Minimax

Weighs the AI's next move against your best possible response before committing.

α β

Alpha-Beta
