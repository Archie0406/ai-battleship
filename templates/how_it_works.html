How the AI Thinks

From the moment you fire, the AI runs a real reasoning pipeline to decide where to shoot next. Here's the order it happens in.

01

Observe

Every shot you take, and every shot the AI takes, updates a shared knowledge state: which cells are unknown, missed, hit, or fully sunk.

02

Score every remaining cell

For each ship still afloat, the AI checks every legal placement on the board and tallies which unfired cells that placement would cover — cells covered by more possible placements score higher.

03

Weight active hits

If a ship has already been hit but not sunk, placements that would explain that hit get extra weight, so the AI naturally hunts along a wounded ship instead of firing randomly.

04

Fire on the highest-scoring cell

The AI takes its shot, the result updates the knowledge state, and — on a hit — it immediately fires again, repeating the loop until it misses.

05

Formal reasoning (upcoming)

Later phases replace and extend this heuristic with a formal CSP + backtracking solver, forward-chaining inference, and sampled minimax evaluation — visible live on the AI Lab page.
